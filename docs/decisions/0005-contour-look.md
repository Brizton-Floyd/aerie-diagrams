# 0005 Contour: the visual identity

Status: accepted, 2026-10-22

## Decision

Aerie looks like **Contour**: the system as surveyed terrain seen from above.

- Paper and ink, with sage contour bands that deepen as the reader zooms in.
- External systems are hatched.
- Scenarios draw themselves as a red trail across the architecture.
- Type: Source Serif 4 and Source Sans 3 (element names in Sans); the engineer audience speaks in
  Source Code Pro.
- Wording stays at 13.5 px or larger before any fit-to-screen scaling.
- Motion is an unhurried glide of about 560 ms, with a reduced-motion alternative.

## Why

The captain chose Contour from three prototyped directions. It is calm, legible and our own, and the
terrain metaphor matches zoom: going deeper means going down a contour.

## Consequences

- Icons are our own.
- A per-service colour option may come later as an author choice; it is not part of v1.
